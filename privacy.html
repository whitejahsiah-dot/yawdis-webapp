1. Who we are

YAWDI's ("Yawdi's", "we", "us" or "our") is a Canadian food business. This policy explains how we handle personal information when you visit yawdi.ca (the "Site"), comment on our recipes and posts, contact us, or buy our products.

We follow Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and, where they apply to you, the privacy laws of your province, including Quebec's Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector. By using the Site you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please do not use the features that collect personal information, such as commenting.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Comments. When you comment on a recipe or post, we collect the name you enter (optional; "Anonymous" is used if you leave it blank), your comment, the post it belongs to, and the date and time. We do not ask for your email address to comment. Please don't include personal details such as your phone number or address in a comment.
  • Messages to us. Our contact form opens your own email program with your message ready to send to vgeorge@yawdi.ca. The Site does not store what you type. When you send the email, we receive your name, email address and whatever you write.
  • Orders. When you buy from us, Shopify collects the details needed to complete your order (see Purchases through Shopify).

Information collected automatically

  • Technical information. Like every website, our hosting and security providers automatically receive your IP address, browser and device type, the pages you request and the date and time. They use this to deliver pages, keep the Site secure and block attacks and spam.
  • Information stored on your own device. The Site saves a few small items in your browser so it works properly: your cart, which posts you have liked, and whether you closed an announcement. This stays on your device and is not sent to us (see Cookies and browser storage).

Your location (only if you ask)

On our Find Us page you can choose "Use my location" to sort stores by distance. Your browser will ask your permission first. Your location is used only on your device to sort the list and centre the map. We do not send it to our servers or store it. Google Maps loads the map for that area, as it would for any area you scroll to.

What we don't collect

We do not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels, or analytics tools that follow you across websites. We do not collect sensitive information such as health, financial or government ID details, and we ask that you don't share it with us.

3. How we use your information

We use personal information only for the purposes below, and with your consent, which you give by choosing to use these features:

  • to publish your approved comments and show likes on recipes and posts;
  • to moderate comments and keep the community safe and respectful;
  • to answer your questions, wholesale and press inquiries;
  • to process, ship and support your orders, and handle returns and refunds;
  • to send you news and offers by email, only if you opted in (you can unsubscribe at any time using the link in any message, in line with Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation);
  • to operate, secure and improve the Site, and prevent fraud and abuse;
  • to meet our legal, tax and accounting obligations.

We will not use your information for a new purpose without telling you and, where required, asking for your consent.

4. AI-assisted comment moderation

To keep our comment sections safe, every new comment is checked by an automated moderation tool before it can appear publicly. Here is exactly how it works:

  • What is checked: the text of your comment is sent to the OpenAI Moderation service, operated by OpenAI, L.L.C. in the United States. The tool screens text for categories such as harassment, hate, threats, sexual content, violence and self-harm. Your name is not part of what is screened.
  • Possible results:
    • Approved automatically: no concerns detected, so the comment is published;
    • Held for review: a possible concern was detected, so the comment stays hidden until a member of our team reviews it. When this happens, our team receives an email containing your name and comment so they can review it;
    • Rejected automatically: the tool flagged the comment as breaking its content rules, so it is not published. This decision is made by the automated tool alone, without a person reviewing it first.
  • Human review and your rights: if your comment is not published and you think that's a mistake, email vgeorge@yawdi.ca with the post and roughly when you commented. A member of our team will review the decision. You can also ask what information was used and the main reasons for the decision, and you can correct the information.
  • How OpenAI handles it: OpenAI processes comment text on our behalf as a service provider. Under OpenAI's business terms, data sent through its API is not used to train its models by default, and may be kept for a limited time for abuse and misuse monitoring. See OpenAI's privacy policy.
  • Our team has the final say: staff can approve, reject or delete any comment at any time, and may remove comments that break our community standards even after they were published.

Approved comments, including the name you entered, are public and can be seen by anyone who visits the Site.

5. Purchases through Shopify

Our online store and checkout are provided by Shopify Inc. When you check out, you enter your contact details, shipping and billing address, phone number (optional) and payment information on Shopify's secure checkout pages. Payments are processed by Shopify and its payment partners (for example Shop Pay, PayPal or Google Pay, if you choose them). We never see or store your full card number.

We receive the order details we need to fulfil and support your purchase: your name, email address, shipping address, phone number if you provided it, and what you bought. Shopify's handling of your information is described in Shopify's privacy policy, and any payment method you choose has its own privacy terms.

8. Storage and processing outside Canada

Some of our service providers store or process information outside Canada, mainly in the United States. That information is protected by our agreements with them, but it may be accessible to courts, law enforcement and national security authorities in those countries under their laws. If you have questions about how your information is handled outside Canada, contact us using the details below.

9. When we share information

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. We share it only:

  • with the service providers listed above, so they can provide their services to us;
  • publicly, for comments you post once they are approved;
  • when required by law, such as a court order or a valid request from authorities, or to protect the rights, safety or property of Yawdi's, our customers or others;
  • with a buyer or successor if our business is sold or reorganised, in which case your information would remain protected by this policy.

10. How long we keep it

  • Comments: kept while they serve the community. Comments that are rejected or deleted are removed when our team clears them, and you can ask us to delete your comments at any time.
  • Emails you send us: kept as long as needed to answer you and keep a reasonable record of our conversation, then deleted.
  • Order records: kept for as long as Canadian tax and accounting rules require (generally six years).
  • Technical logs held by our providers: kept for short periods set by those providers.

When we no longer need personal information, we delete it or make it anonymous.

11. Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect personal information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) across the Site, access controls so that only authorised staff can see unpublished comments, and reputable providers with strong security practices. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a privacy breach creates a real risk of significant harm to you, we will notify you and the appropriate privacy regulator as the law requires.

12. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you have the right to:

  • access the personal information we hold about you and learn how it has been used and shared;
  • correct information that is inaccurate or incomplete;
  • withdraw your consent, for example by unsubscribing from emails (this may limit what we can do for you, such as answering a question);
  • ask us to delete your comments or other personal information, unless we must keep it by law;
  • ask for a human review of an automated comment decision (see section 4);
  • where applicable, receive your information in a commonly used format.

To make a request, email vgeorge@yawdi.ca. We may need to confirm your identity first. We will reply within 30 days. There is no charge for a reasonable request.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada. If you live in Quebec, you can also contact the Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec.

13. Children

The Site is meant for adults and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, or from children under 14 in Quebec, without a parent's or guardian's consent. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

14. Other websites

The Site links to other websites and services, such as Instagram, Facebook, press articles and retailers. We are not responsible for their privacy practices. Please read their policies before giving them any information.

15. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the Site or the law changes. We will post the new version here and update the date at the top. If we make an important change to how we use your personal information, we will make it clear on the Site and, where required, ask for your consent.
