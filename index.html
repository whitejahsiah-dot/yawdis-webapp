Yawdi's was born from a love of bold Caribbean cooking and a desire to share authentic island flavours with every kitchen. Each jar is made in Canada using domestic and imported ingredients selected for flavour and quality.

From the smoky depth of real Scotch Bonnet peppers to the floral sweetness of lavender, every product is crafted to make everyday cooking feel like a celebration.
